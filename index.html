How the numbers work

FP/G is a projected stat line scored with your league's scoring. The stat line is a blend of Sleeper's season projection and last season's per-game stats; set the mix under Settings. Other sites' fantasy-point totals are never used, only their raw stats.

VORP (value over replacement): every team's 10 starting slots (PG, SG, G, SF, PF, F, C, 3×UTIL) are filled league-wide with the best players. The best player left over at each position sets that position's replacement level. A player's VORP is how far he sits above it. With 3 UTIL slots, 100 starters league-wide decide replacement level.

Gain is how much your roster's value goes up if you draft him now. It uses an optimal lineup of your current players, with bench players counting at the bench weight. Once your C and UTIL spots are full, another center's Gain drops even if his VORP is high.

Now is Gain minus what you'd lose by passing on him, using Sleeper ADP to estimate whether he will still be there at your next pick. Two similar players? Take the one who won't make it back. The top three by this column are shown as recommendations.

Next is the chance a player is still available at your next pick (ADP ± a spread that grows later in the draft).

+/-: ADP rank minus our rank. Big positive numbers are players your scoring likes more than the market does, so you can often wait on them. Negative numbers are players the market will overdraft for your format.

During the draft

  • Live sync polls the Sleeper draft every few seconds and removes drafted players.
  • Offline or a draft on another site? Use Taken / Mine on each row.
  • Start mock draft simulates the other 9 teams picking around ADP so you can practise from pick 6.
